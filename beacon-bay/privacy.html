Beacon Bay – Privacy Policy

Effective 1 October 2026

Beacon Bay is a logic puzzle game for Android made by Ahmed Itani ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game handles, why, and the choices you have. We wrote it to be short and plain.

The short version

  • There are no accounts. We never ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos or location.
  • Your progress, settings, shells and the items you own are stored only on your device.
  • The game shows ads through Google AdMob, sends anonymous gameplay statistics and crash reports through Google Firebase, and sells optional items through Google Play.
  • You can turn off gameplay statistics at any time in Settings → Share anonymous stats. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland you can change your ad choices in Settings → Manage ad consent.

Information stored on your device

Your puzzle progress, stars, streaks, shells, unlocked looks, settings and a small queue of recent gameplay events are saved in the game's private storage on your device. We cannot see this data. It is removed when you use Settings → Reset progress (which keeps purchases) or when you uninstall the game. Android backup of this data is turned off.

Information shared with service providers

Advertising – Google AdMob

Free players see occasional ads between puzzles and can choose to watch a rewarded ad for shells. To show and measure ads, Google AdMob collects device and network information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device model, operating system, language, and how you interact with ads. Ads are requested with a general-audience (G) content rating.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the game shows Google's consent form before any ads load, and you can change your choice later in Settings → Manage ad consent. Elsewhere you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your Android device settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads). Beacon Bay Plus removes all ads.

Learn how Google uses this information: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Gameplay statistics – Google Analytics for Firebase

If "Share anonymous stats" is on (the default), the game sends events such as "puzzle started", "puzzle completed", "hint used", the puzzle size and mode, and settings changes, together with an app-instance identifier, device model, operating system version, language and approximate country (derived from your IP address). We use this only to understand which puzzles are too hard or too easy and to improve the game. We do not link it to the advertising ID and we do not use it for advertising. Turning the setting off stops collection straight away.

Crash reports – Firebase Crashlytics

If the game crashes, Crashlytics sends a crash report: the stack trace, device model, operating system version, app version, free memory and disk space, and the screen the game was on. Crash reports follow the same "Share anonymous stats" setting.

Purchases – Google Play

Optional purchases (Beacon Bay Plus, hint packs, shell packs and cosmetic bundles) are processed entirely by Google Play. We never see your card or payment details. Google tells the game only which item was bought, so it can be delivered and restored.

Connectivity check

To avoid waiting on ads when you are offline, the game occasionally contacts connectivitycheck.gstatic.com, a Google address that returns an empty response. Nothing about you is sent.

Google's own privacy policy applies to the information these services collect: policies.google.com/privacy. Firebase privacy information: firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, files or location.
  • We do not use loot boxes, energy timers or paid level skips.

Retention

Google Analytics keeps gameplay statistics for no longer than 14 months. Crash reports are kept by Crashlytics for 90 days. Advertising data is retained according to Google's policies.

Children

Beacon Bay is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the game, contact us and we will help remove it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, Canada's PIPEDA or California's CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information, to object to or restrict processing, and to withdraw consent. Because we hold no account data, most of these choices are available directly in the game and in your device settings. For anything else, email us and we will respond within 30 days. You may also complain to your local data-protection authority.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above, and for significant changes we will tell you in the game.
