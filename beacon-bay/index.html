Beacon Bay

A calm logic puzzle game for Android: one lighthouse per bay, none in the same row or column, none touching.

Delete your data: Beacon Bay has no accounts. Your progress lives only on your phone; use Settings → Reset progress or uninstall the game to remove it. Turning off Settings → Share anonymous stats stops statistics and crash reports straight away, and what was already sent is deleted automatically (statistics within 14 months, crash reports within 90 days). For any other deletion request, email us with the words "Beacon Bay data deletion" and we will remove everything we can identify within 30 days.
